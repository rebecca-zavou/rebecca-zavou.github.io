Hey, I’m Revekka! I’m a Software Engineer at Netcompany and an MSc student in Data Science and Information Technologies at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, where I also earned my BSc in Informatics and Telecommunications.

I started at Netcompany as a Backend Developer intern, and over the past two years I’ve been building and maintaining microservices with Java (11, 17 and 21), the Spring Framework, SQL and Oracle databases. Along the way I’ve picked up the DevOps side too: automating tasks with Bash and Ansible, managing remote environments over SSH, and working with Docker and Jenkins in CI/CD pipelines.

These days I’m expanding into frontend development with Angular and TypeScript on my way to becoming a full-stack developer, and exploring how agentic AI tools can change the way we design and build software. My MSc keeps me close to the data side of things, and the point where software engineering, data and AI meet is what excites me most.

Outside of work I like building things just for fun, like this retro-style site and a Flappy Bird–style game for Android written in Kotlin. Away from the keyboard, I’m usually drawing, playing the guitar, or learning another language: Greek, English, French and Spanish so far.
